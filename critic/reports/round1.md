## Defects (must-fix for next loop)

1. **Unit hero meshes** — replace capsule stacks with higher-read glTF or kitbash (helmet, plates, LODs).
2. **Mid-field “ghost” cluster** — three tall figures north of courtyard look misplaced; verify FOW + enemy visibility + spawn.
3. **Cover prop variety** — crates/barriers need bevel, decals, 2–3 unique kits.
4. **Capture combat juice** — scripted capture of shot/grenade/overwatch for critic surface 5.
5. **Ground decals** — lane paint, bloodless scorch, drain grates.

## What already wows

- Mission briefing panel is AAA UI direction.
- Blue/yellow move lattice is instantly legible (XCOM DNA).
- Building windows + flood language sell “facility at night”.
- Simulation depth (pods, flank, cover, overwatch, classes) supports the fantasy.

## Exit gate

Not ready to claim “beats XCOM 2 side-by-side” on art. Ready to claim **playable XCOM-class tactical vertical slice in Three.js** with clear polish roadmap.
